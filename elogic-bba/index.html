About the programme

A BBA designed backwards from what graduates can do

Years 1 and 2 build a broad foundation across management, economics, accounting, marketing, finance, people and analytics. In Year 3 every student specialises, completes a summer internship and consults for a real client.

  • Small sections of 20, so every student speaks, presents and gets feedback
  • Four specialisations: Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Business Analytics
  • A faculty mentor for every student, and two Parent-Teacher Meetings a year

Pedagogy

How we teach

Roughly a third of class time is spent on concepts, a third on practice and a third on applying ideas to real organisations.

Case method

Read a business case before class; debate the decision in class. Used most in Strategy, Marketing and Finance.

Flipped classroom

Short videos and readings at home; class time for problems and discussion. Economics, Statistics, Accounting.

Workshops and studios

Hands-on group sessions: role plays, design sprints, coding labs. Communication, Design Thinking, Analytics.

Business simulation

Teams run a simulated company over eight rounds in the Semester 4 Business Simulation Workshop.

Live projects

Briefs from partner companies, start-ups and NGOs, with a final presentation to the client.

Mentoring

Every student has a faculty mentor (about ten mentees each) who meets them monthly and talks to parents.

Sessions per credit

One session is a 60-minute contact hour. Practice-heavy subjects need more contact time per credit.

Assessment

How students are evaluated

Exam

40% internal (two quizzes 10%, assignment 10%, mid-term 20%) + 60% end-term exam of 3 hours.

Workshop

100% continuous: studio exercises 50%, participation and peer review 20%, final showcase 30%.

Project

50% internal (proposal, milestones, mentor) + 50% final report and viva.

Outcomes

    Business knowledge

    Critical thinking
